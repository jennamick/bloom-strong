Meet Colette

Colette Bennett

Certified Personal Trainer

Hi, I’m Colette, a certified personal trainer in St. Petersburg, Florida. I built Bloom Strong on one belief: strength doesn’t require a starting line.

I blend practical strength training with consistent accountability to support beginners and busy professionals. Whether you’re taking your first step or finding your way back to fitness, we’ll start with your goals and current fitness level.

My approach is simple: learn the movements, find a manageable routine, and build from there. You’ll have guidance and encouragement along the way.
