Frequently asked questions

Select a question to read the answer.

Do I need experience to get started?

No prior strength-training experience is needed. Beginner Strength is designed for people who are new to training or returning after a break, with an emphasis on learning proper form and progressing at a manageable pace.

What training options are available?

Bloom Strong offers Personal Training, Beginner Strength, and Virtual Coaching. Compare the training options to learn more.

Where does training take place?

Bloom Strong is based in St. Petersburg, Florida, and also offers virtual coaching. The specific location and available session times should be confirmed before booking.

Can I train from home?

Yes. Virtual coaching includes personalized workouts, virtual check-ins, and encouragement. Discuss your available space and equipment when exploring a plan.

What equipment will I need?

Equipment needs depend on your training plan and whether you train in person or online. Discuss what you already have before purchasing anything.

What happens during a consultation?

The free consultation is an opportunity to discuss your goals, current fitness level, schedule, and training preferences. You can also ask about pricing and availability before choosing a next step.

Where can I find pricing and book a session?

Prices are not yet published on this website. Email Colette to ask about pricing, availability, and a free consultation.
